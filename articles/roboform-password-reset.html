Blog / RoboForm Password Reset

Review safe steps for changing or resetting your RoboForm account password.

Start with the official sign-in page

Open RoboForm by typing its official website address yourself or by using the installed application. Check the address bar carefully before entering your Master Password. Avoid sign-in links in unexpected emails, pop-ups or messages.

Check the account details

Confirm that you are using the correct RoboForm account and that the keyboard layout, Caps Lock and password manager settings are not changing what you type. If you recently changed your Master Password, make sure you are not using an older saved value.

Use the available recovery guidance

RoboForm uses a Master Password to protect encrypted data. Recovery options can be limited by the product's security design, so do not assume support can reveal or reset a forgotten Master Password. Follow the current instructions in RoboForm's official help center.

Protect your credentials

Never share your Master Password, recovery information or one-time verification codes with someone claiming to provide support. If you suspect that your account or device is compromised, use official RoboForm guidance to secure it.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I get RoboForm help?

Start with the official RoboForm website and support center. Confirm that the address is genuine before signing in or sharing account details.

Can support tell me my Master Password?

Your Master Password protects encrypted information. Do not expect a support representative to know or disclose it. Follow RoboForm's current security and recovery guidance.

What details should I provide when reporting a problem?

Describe the device, operating system, browser, RoboForm version, error message and troubleshooting steps. Keep passwords and verification codes private.
